What is Balatro?

Balatro is a card game in which you combine poker hands to earn points, but with a strategic and addictive style. As you progress, you get special cards that change the rules and help you improve your score. Although it is based on poker, it is a solitary, fast game and very different from traditional poker.



How to play Balatro?

Balatro is a card game where you combine poker hands (such as pairs, straights, or full houses) to earn points and advance through rounds. In each round, you must defeat three blinds: the Small Blind, the Big Blind, and the Boss Blind, which are scoring targets. Each one has a multiplier that means you need more points to beat them, but the Boss Blind is not only more difficult, it also has a special negative effect (for example, blocking cards or hands). At the end of each blind, you can enter the store, where you spend money to buy upgrades and plan your strategy. There you can get:

Wild Cards (Jokers): these give passive effects, such as point multipliers, combos, or rule changes.

Tarot Cards: these alter individual cards, for example, doubling their value or making them stronger.

Planetary Cards: improve a specific play (such as a trio giving many more points).

Spectral Cards: usually have rare or powerful effects, but often with risks.

Vouchers: unlock permanent upgrades, new cards, or rules that appear in future games.

There are also tags that can be obtained randomly and that change the style of play, such as giving you more cards, more money, fewer hands per round, etc. To get them, you must skip the blinds, but by doing so you will not be able to access the store. The Boss Blind cannot be skipped. To win the game, you have to beat the Boss Blind in round 8, which is special because it has a much more difficult modifier. Finally, the game has an infinite mode where you can keep playing without a round limit until you can't beat a blind. Balatro mixes chance, strategy, and quick decisions, and although it is based on poker, you don't need to know how to play poker to enjoy it.
